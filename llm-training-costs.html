Training an LLM: hardware and people drive the bill

Eight stages from raw text to a deployed model, the four resources they consume, and what six well-known models used.

The eight stages of LLM training and how heavily each uses data, compute, energy, and people and time, rated none, low, medium, or high.

Resource use
Author’s rating; three bars = heaviest use
1Collect
Crawl the web; license books, code, and articles.
2Clean
Remove junk and private data; split text into tokens.
3Design
Choose an architecture; run smaller experiments.
4Pretrain
Predict the next token across trillions of tokens, for weeks.
5Evaluate
Score against benchmarks and past runs.
6Fine-tune
Learn from human examples and rankings.
7Safety test
Red-team for harmful output; fix what turns up.
8Deploy

Four resources Share of development cost1

Data

Coverage and quality limit what a model can learn. Llama 3.1: 15T+ tokens.10

Not itemized in these estimates

Compute

Allows larger models and more experiments. GPT-4’s hardware: about $800M to buy.1

47–64%

Energy

A small share of cost, but power limits cluster size. GPT-3 to Llama 3.1: about 17× more.5,10

2–6%

People & time

Experts turn compute into a better, safer model. Llama 2: 350+ red-teamers.11

29–49%
