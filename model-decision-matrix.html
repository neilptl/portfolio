How to read the bars: more bars = better for deployment. Size small <50 MB, medium <1 GB. Accuracy top = within 1 point of the best on that benchmark, good = within 5. Speed fast <15 ms, moderate <100 ms. Explainability high = exact reasons per prediction, partial = approximate maps, low = outputs only. ★ Start here = the smallest model near the best.

Speed measured on an AMD Ryzen 5 7600 CPU, one input at a time. SST-2 and census-income (OpenML Adult) accuracy: my runs. ImageNet and MMLU: published. Tabular models train on your data instead of arriving pre-trained. *Estimate: 16 GB read per token at 1 TB/s. Method and sources: portfolio page.
